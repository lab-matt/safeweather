# Reference beam cases

Use E = 29,000 ksi and I = 204 in⁴ for the illustrative W12×26. Loads below exclude self-weight. These reference values are derived from standard elastic beam formulas; they have not yet been compared with a browser execution of the app.

1. Simply supported, 20 ft, 1 kip/ft over the full span: reactions 10 kip each; maximum moment wL²/8 = 50 kip-ft; maximum deflection 5wL⁴/(384EI) = 0.608519 in.
2. Cantilever fixed at the left, 20 ft, 1 kip/ft over the full span: vertical reaction 20 kip, internal fixed-end moment −wL²/2 = −200 kip-ft; tip deflection wL⁴/(8EI) = 5.841785 in.
3. Simply supported, 20 ft, 1 kip/ft from 5 to 15 ft: reactions 5 kip each and maximum moment 37.5 kip-ft at midspan.
4. Zero load: reactions, moment, shear, and deflection are zero for either support condition.

For the steel example, the additional safety factor changes only the 33 ksi illustrative bending limit: factor 2 gives 16.5 ksi. Deflection limit for 20 ft is 240/360 = 0.666667 in.
